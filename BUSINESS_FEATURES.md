## 🏭 2. Warehouse & Field Operations

### Geo-Fenced Attendance (Remote Login + Tracking)
| Feature | How It Works | Business Value |
|---------|--------------|----------------|
| **Geo-Fence Check-In** | Staff must be within 50m of warehouse/site to clock in | Eliminates buddy-punching, ghost employees |
| **Biometric + GPS** | Face ID + location verification | 100% authentic attendance |
| **Offline-First** | Works without internet — syncs when online | Construction sites, basements, remote yards |
| **Geo-Fence Alerts** | Auto-alert if staff leaves zone during shift | Security, compliance, accountability |
| **Shift Rules** | Grace periods, early/late thresholds, overtime auto-calc | Payroll accuracy, labor law compliance |
| **Multi-Site** | One app, multiple warehouses/projects | Centralized workforce management |

| **Gantt + Dependencies** | Critical path, slack, resource leveling — on-time delivery |
